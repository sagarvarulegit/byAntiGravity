A bulb is rated 220V, 100W. When operated on 110V, what is the power consumed?
A) 100W
B) 75W
C) 50W
D) 25W
💡 Filament Resistance (R) is Constant!
P = V² / R ➔ Power ∝ V²
⚡ Depends on V²
Step 1: Voltage 220V ➔ 110V (½)
Step 2: Square (½)² = ¼
Step 3: New Power 100W × ¼ = 25W ✅
