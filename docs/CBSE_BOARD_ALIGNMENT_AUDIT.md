# CBSE Board Alignment Audit

**Date:** June 24, 2026  
**Source:** Official 2026 board papers, 2025–26 sample papers & marking schemes, official 2025–26 curriculum  
**Method:** Manual comparison against platform content (chapter packs, quizzes, revision notes, lesson-wise content)

---

## Executive Summary

Chapter-pack coverage is **fairly strong** for Mathematics, Science, and Social Science. However, the platform is **not yet fully board-paper aligned**.

The largest problem is not missing syllabus content — it is **missing exam-format preparation**: descriptive answers, proofs, case studies, diagrams, maps, source questions, writing skills, and grammar.

| Subject | Syllabus Coverage | Board-Paper Readiness |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | Strong | Partial |
| Science | Strong | Partial |
| Social Science | Strong, with organisation issues | Partial |
| English Language & Literature | Major gaps | Low |
| Hindi Course B | Major gaps | Low |
| Health & Physical Education | Not a comparable board paper | Supplementary |

---

## Mathematics — Codes 041 and 241

### What matches
- All 14 current Mathematics chapters have chapter packs
- Chapter quizzes exist
- Real Numbers has detailed lesson-wise notes
- Chapter list matches the rationalised CBSE syllabus

### What is missing
- Most chapters have no lesson-wise notes
- Current quizzes mainly test selectable answers (MCQs)

**Official paper format contains:**
- 20 one-mark MCQ / assertion-reason questions
- Five 2-mark questions
- Six 3-mark questions
- Four 5-mark questions
- Three case studies
- Proofs, graphical solutions, multi-step application problems

### Decision needed
Does the platform prepare students for:
- **Mathematics Standard (Code 041)**
- **Mathematics Basic (Code 241)**
- **Both**, with difficulty-labelled questions?

## Science — Code 086

### What matches
- All 13 Science chapters have chapter packs and quizzes
- Biology, Chemistry, Physics all covered
- Chemical Reactions content maps directly to exam topics (balancing, reaction types, observations, redox, displacement, decomposition)

### What is missing
- Only Real Numbers and part of Chemical Reactions have proper lesson-wise notes
- Chapter-pack summaries + MCQ quizzes are insufficient

**Official paper: 39 questions across Biology, Chemistry, Physics including:**
- Assertion-reason questions
- Experimental observations
- Balanced equations
- Numerical calculations
- Diagrams and labelled figures
- Genetics crosses
- Circuit and ray-diagram reasoning
- Activity-based questions
- 4- and 5-mark integrated case questions

### Needed additions
- Diagram practice
- Experiment/observation questions
- Numericals
- "Give reason" answers
- Case-based questions
- 2-, 3- and 5-mark answer practice

---

## Social Science — Code 087

### What matches
- 22 chapter packs: History (5), Geography (7), Political Science (5), Economics (5)
- Broadly covers the complete textbook collection

### Problems found

**Organisation:**
Social Science is represented under separate subject names (History, Geography, Civics, Economics) instead of as one unified board subject. These appear as separate platform subjects.

**Question format:**
Current quizzes do not reproduce the official paper format.

**Official paper contains:**
- Four 20-mark sections
- MCQs and assertion-reason
- Picture/cartoon interpretation
- Source-based questions
- 2-, 3- and 5-mark answers
- Map identification and labelling
- Data and situation analysis

### Priority missing areas
- Dedicated CBSE map-work practice
- Cartoon and image interpretation
- Source/case extracts
- Structured 2/3/5-mark model answers
- Chapter assessment-scope labels (board-exam, periodic-only, project-only)

## English Language & Literature — Code 184

**This is the most serious mismatch.**

### Official paper structure
| Section | Marks |
|---|---|
| Reading Skills | 20 |
| Grammar and Creative Writing | 20 |
| Literature | 40 |

### Current platform coverage
The platform concentrates mainly on prose and supplementary literature.

### Missing or weak areas
- Unseen reading comprehension
- Grammar practice (tenses, modals, subject-verb agreement, reported speech, etc.)
- Letter writing (formal/informal)
- Analytical paragraphs
- Poetry coverage:
  - The Trees
  - The Ball Poem
  - Dust of Snow
  - For Anne Gregory
  - A Tiger in the Zoo
  - The Tale of Custard the Dragon
- Literary devices
- Comparison questions across two texts
- 40–50 word answers
- 100–120 word analytical answers

**Verdict:** English cannot currently be described as complete CBSE board preparation.

## Hindi Course B — Code 085

### Official paper structure
| Section | Marks |
|---|---|
| अपठित बोध (Unseen Comprehension) | 14 |
| व्यावहारिक व्याकरण (Applied Grammar) | 16 |
| पाठ्यपुस्तक एवं पूरक पाठ्यपुस्तक (Textbook & Supplementary) | 28 |
| रचनात्मक लेखन (Creative Writing) | 22 |

### Current platform coverage
Content corresponds to Hindi Course B but mainly contains selected literature chapters.

### Major missing areas
- Unseen passages (अपठित बोध)
- Grammar: पदबंध, वाक्य रूपांतरण, समास, मुहावरे
- अनुच्छेद लेखन (paragraph writing)
- Formal/informal letter formats
- सूचना, विज्ञापन and email-style tasks
- Several prescribed prose, poetry and supplementary lessons
- Extract-based poetry questions
- Longer analytical literature answers

**Verdict:** Like English, Hindi currently covers only one portion of the complete paper.

---

## Health & Physical Education

This should **not** be treated as equivalent to Mathematics, Science or Social Science.

CBSE lists Health and Physical Education under **internal assessment**, not as one of the main 80-mark board papers.

**Recommendation:** Label it as Supplementary/Internal Assessment. It should not contribute to any "board syllabus completion" percentage alongside the five core subjects.

---

## Recommended Priority Order

1. **Build an official subject/chapter/question-type coverage matrix**
2. **English** — Add reading comprehension, grammar, writing, and poetry
3. **Hindi** — Add unseen passages, grammar, writing, and missing literature
4. **Mathematics** — Add 2/3/5-mark, case-study and proof questions
5. **Science** — Add diagrams, experiments and numericals
6. **Social Science** — Add map and source-based practice
7. **Generate complete subject-wise 80-mark mock papers**
8. **Label every question with:** Subject, Chapter, Topic, Marks, Question type, Difficulty, Source year/set
