## 💻 3. Remotion Technical Architecture

### 3.1 Core Remotion Primitives
- **Frame-Driven Animation**: Always drive motion using `useCurrentFrame()`, `interpolate()`, and `spring()` for 100% deterministic frame renders.
- **Path Tracing**: Use `strokeDasharray` + `strokeDashoffset` to animate light rays, electric current, and chemical bonds live on screen.
- **Asset Preloading**: Always use `<Img src={staticFile(...)} />` from `"remotion"` to ensure assets are preloaded into memory before rendering.
- **Phonetic Math Sanitizer**: Pass all mathematical formulas through `cleanMathTextForSpeech()` in `generate-voiceovers.js` (e.g. `1/Rp` ➔ *"one over R P"*, `1/f = 1/v - 1/u"` ➔ *"one over f equals one over v minus one over u"*) before TTS generation.
- **Timeless Title Subtitles**: Omit explicit NCERT chapter numbers (e.g. *"Chapter 12"*) in video titles/subtitles to prevent stale branding across textbook editions. Use domain titles like `"CBSE Class 10 Physics • Electricity"`.

## 📖 4. Subject Expansion Roadmap

### ⚡ Physics
1. **Electricity**: Ohm's Law ($V = IR$), Push vs Flow Analogy, Parallel Resistance Trap ($1/R_p$).
2. **Light**: Convex Lens Ray Diagrams, Bicycle Refraction Analogy, Lens Formula ($1/f = 1/v - 1/u$).
3. **Human Eye & Colourful World**: Myopia vs Hypermetropia, Glass Prism Dispersion & Rainbow Formation.

1. **Chemical Reactions & Equations**: Water Electrolysis ($2:1$ gas volume ratio), Thermal Decomposition of $Pb(NO_3)_2$.
2. **Acids, Bases & Salts**: $\text{pH}$ Scale, Neutralization Reactions, Zinc + Acid Hydrogen Gas Test.

### 🫀 Biology
1. **Life Processes**: 4-Chambered Human Heart Double Circulation, Stomatal Opening/Closing Mechanism.
2. **Control & Coordination**: Human Reflex Arc Neuron Impulse Path.
